    About

    Josh Daynard

    Precision and intuition, with room for happenstance.

    Josh photographs from the Santa Cruz Mountains: weirs and piers, strangers mid-gesture, the eye of a mantis, a hawk turning to its mate, a single cloud. He works slowly, waits for the light to do something unrepeatable, and shares what he finds as gifts of light.
